# PRAVAHA Data Manifest & Provenance Log

This manifest tracks every dataset downloaded and processed for the FLOODCAST hackathon.

## Data Integrity Standard
- **Zero Synthetic Data**: All training and validation data are strictly real historical records acquired from official USGS NWIS, USGS StreamStats / GAGES-II, and Open-Meteo archive APIs.
- **Read-Only Raw Data**: Files in `data/raw/` are immutable upon download.
- **Reproducibility**: Each record logs source URL, parameters, timestamp, row count, date range, and SHA-256 hash.
