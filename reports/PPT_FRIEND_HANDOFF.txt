2. THE 5-SLIDE NARRATIVE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 1 — THE PROBLEM: The Generalisation Crisis in River Discharge Forecasting
- Focus: Why standard ML fails when transferring to unseen river systems.
- Key concepts: Catchment scale mismatch (100x), extreme peak rarity (<2%), 
  advective network leakage in traditional validation.
- Visual: High-contrast conceptual split diagram (calibrated basin vs unseen basin).

SLIDE 2 — OUR SOLUTION: Physics-Informed Specific Discharge & Truth Engine
- Focus: How PRAVAHA solves the scale mismatch and guarantees scientific honesty.
- Key formula: Specific Discharge q = Q / Drainage Area, transformed by log(q + 1).
- Result: +348% R² improvement over raw models (0.1095 -> 0.4910).
- Visual: Screenshot cutout of 01_dashboard.png highlighting the hydrograph curve 
  and the Sacramento Historical Data Limitation Banner.

SLIDE 3 — DATA FOUNDATION & ARCHITECTURE: 100% Real Hydrology & Modular Full-Stack
- Focus: Zero synthetic data, federal data sources, and live backend-frontend flow.
- Key metrics: 170,817 real daily records, 14 years (2010–2023), 34 USGS stations, 
  23 river systems, 25/25 automated backend tests passing in 0.27s.
- Visual: Two-column layout (Data scale cards on left, clean architecture diagram on right).

SLIDE 4 — MACHINE LEARNING RESULTS: Rigorous Evaluation Across Unseen River Systems (LORSO)
- Focus: Decisive benchmark performance under 23-fold Leave-One-River-System-Out cross-validation.
- Winner 1 (Overall): LightGBM Log-Specific Discharge (Pooled R² = 0.4910, RMSE = 82,871 cfs, MAE = 24,644 cfs).
- Winner 2 (Extreme Peaks): LightGBM Linear Specific Discharge (Peak RMSE = 155,136 cfs).
- Forensic insight: Mississippi at Vicksburg accounts for 81.8% of pooled squared error; 
  median station MAE across all other 32 rivers is just 2,623 cfs.
- Visual: Clean benchmark comparison table with green highlight tags for winning metrics.

SLIDE 5 — WORKING PRODUCT, HONEST IMPACT & ROADMAP: Science to Decision Support

- Closing: "PREDICT THE SURGE. PROTECT THE PEOPLE."
